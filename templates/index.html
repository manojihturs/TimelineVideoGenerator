    Pull data — Budget & Box office

    TMDb often has no Budget/Box office figures for Indian regional films. This looks each movie up via Gemini (when GEMINI_API_KEY is configured, falling back to Wikipedia otherwise) and fills in only the blank cells in Movie DB/<name>/Data/data.csv — anything TMDb already filled in is left alone. Budget is formatted Indian-style (₹10 Cr / ₹90 Lakhs).
